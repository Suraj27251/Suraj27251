01 — About

Root cause first,
solution second.

Most problems are symptoms. I work backwards from the symptom until I find the process, data or system decision that caused it.

I help businesses find operational problems, understand the root cause, and turn them into practical improvements through better processes, systems, data and automation.

Over 5+ years in business analysis and operations I've worked hands-on with ERP and CRM platforms, run requirements analysis end to end, mapped processes in BPMN, built analytics that leadership actually reads, and automated the repetitive work that quietly drains operations teams.

I don't jump straight to automation. A 40% efficiency gain usually starts with removing a redundant approval step, not with a new tool — so I look at the whole picture before recommending anything.
